# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the High Performance Tool project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

We value constructive collaboration and contributions that help improve the project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Accepting differing opinions and discussing them respectfully.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Trolling, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards when needed. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, contributions, or other participation that does not follow this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository.

Please include relevant context and links when possible. Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as circumstances allow.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.
